## The sleeve notes

> Pondwater Blue
>
> The Marshlight Trio — tenor sax, upright bass, brushes on snare — play where reeds meet still water at dusk. The sax leans low and unhurried, the bass walks the mudline, the drums fall like rain on leaves. Three frogs, one long evening, in no hurry to leave it. You can hear the room in it: wood, water, and the low hum of evening.

One album title plus a short back-cover blurb, 347 characters in total: the Marshlight Trio played straight as a working band — tenor sax, upright bass, brushes on snare — in the room where the reeds meet still water at dusk.

## How it works

`main.py` (Python stdlib only, no network) writes that exact title and blurb to `/output/entry.txt`. It asserts the entry is 1–400 characters before writing.

Run it like the grader:

```sh
python3 -P -s main.py
```
